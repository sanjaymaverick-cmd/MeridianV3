How to read this desk

Simple words. Exact numbers.

What the words mean

Review (not an order)
A written look. You accept, dismiss, snooze, or do nothing. It never buys or sells by itself.
Paper fill
A pretend trade on the ₹5,000 paper book so we can see if the idea was good.
Live fill
A real ticket, only if you armed live and the signal is very strong. Needs a broker plugin.
Daily PnL
What one quiet day does. Mostly time (theta).
Gamma Scalping PnL
Extra profit or loss from a market jump. Long gamma can help. Short gamma can hurt.
Drawdown pause
If the live book is down 20% from its best, new live trades wait. Open trades may stay.

Start here

  1. Seed the demo desk.
  2. Run one cycle. Every name is paper-traded if the engine likes it.
  3. Read the review cards. They will say “(not an order)”.
  4. Import a real statement (PDF / Excel / photo) and check the rows.
  5. Leave live disarmed until a broker adapter is plugged in.
