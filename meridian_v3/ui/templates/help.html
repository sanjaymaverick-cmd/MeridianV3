How to read this desk

Simple words. Exact numbers.

What the words mean

Review (not an order)
A written look. You accept, dismiss, snooze, or do nothing. It never buys or sells by itself.
Paper fill
A pretend trade on the ₹50,000 paper book so we can see if the idea was good.
Live fill
A real ticket, only if you armed live and the signal is very strong. Needs a broker plugin.
Daily PnL
What one quiet day does. Mostly time (theta).
Gamma Scalping PnL
Extra profit or loss from a market jump. Long gamma can help. Short gamma can hurt.
Drawdown pause
If the live book is down 20% from its best, new live trades wait. Open trades may stay.
Brokerage / GST / STT
Every fill is billed like an Indian contract note. Pick Zerodha, Angel One, or ICICI Direct on Safety. Brokerage is the broker’s line. GST is 18% on brokerage + exchange + SEBI. STT and stamp are statutory. Paper cash is reduced by the full bill. Profit and loss is after fees.

Start here

  1. Seed the demo desk. That also turns paper auto ON.
  2. Leave the window open. About every minute the demo account chooses, buys or exits, and learns.
  3. Read Paper / Live and Decisions. Review cards still say “(not an order)”.
  4. Import a real statement if you want those names watched too.
  5. Leave live disarmed. This loop never sends a real order.
